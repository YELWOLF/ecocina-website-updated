Ecocina Visitor Tracker

Identifies the company of every visitor on your website using their IP address, then pushes them automatically into your lead pipeline.

Step 1 — Get a free IPinfo token

Go to ipinfo.io/signup → create a free account.
Copy your Access Token (looks like abc123def456).

Step 2 — Update the token in the script

Open visitor-tracker.js and replace line 14:

var IPINFO_TOKEN = "YOUR_IPINFO_TOKEN";   // ← paste your token here

Step 3 — Paste this snippet on your website

Add this just before the closing </body> tag on every page of ecocinacom.com:

<!-- Ecocina Visitor Tracker -->
<script>
(function(){var T="YOUR_IPINFO_TOKEN",W="https://spout-hamlet-stapling.ngrok-free.dev/webhook/rb2b-intake",C=7;function k(i){return"ecocina_v_"+i}function s(i){try{var r=localStorage.getItem(k(i));if(!r)return!1;return(Date.now()-parseInt(r,10))<C*864e5}catch(e){return!1}}function m(i){try{localStorage.setItem(k(i),String(Date.now()))}catch(e){}}function p(o){if(!o)return"";return o.replace(/^AS\d+\s+/i,"").trim()}function b(o){var bots=["amazon","google","microsoft","cloudflare","digitalocean","linode","ovh","hetzner","vultr","fastly","akamai"];var l=(o||"").toLowerCase();return bots.some(function(x){return l.indexOf(x)!==-1})}function run(){fetch("https://ipinfo.io/json?token="+T).then(function(r){return r.json()}).then(function(d){var ip=d.ip||"",org=p(d.org),city=d.city||"",country=d.country||"",domain="";if(!ip||b(org)||s(ip))return;if(org)domain=org.toLowerCase().replace(/[^a-z0-9\s]/g,"").trim().split(/\s+/)[0]+".com";fetch(W,{method:"POST",headers:{"Content-Type":"application/json"},body:JSON.stringify({first_name:"Website",last_name:"Visitor",email:"",company_name:org,company_domain:domain,company_industry:"",title:"",page_url:window.location.href,visitor_ip:ip,visitor_city:city,visitor_country:country,source:"ipinfo-tracker"}),keepalive:true}).catch(function(){});m(ip)}).catch(function(){})}if(document.readyState==="loading"){document.addEventListener("DOMContentLoaded",run)}else{setTimeout(run,500)}})();
</script>
Replace YOUR_IPINFO_TOKEN in the snippet above with your actual token before pasting.

What happens after install

  1. Visitor lands on your site → script runs silently after page load
  2. IPinfo returns their company + city + country from their IP
  3. Script pushes to your n8n RB2B webhook
  4. n8n normalizes the lead and fires the full 9-agent pipeline
  5. If qualified → personalized cold email sent automatically

Free tier limits

  • IPinfo: 50,000 lookups/month (free)
  • Cooldown: same IP fires only once per 7 days (stored in localStorage)
  • Bot filter: AWS, Google, Cloudflare etc. are auto-skipped
